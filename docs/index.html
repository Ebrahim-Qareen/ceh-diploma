Course Roadmap

The course follows the attacker's own methodology, in order. Colours mark the five arcs: Foundations · Recon & Scanning · Gaining Access · Post-Exploitation · Applications & Networks.

S01

Foundations, Lab Build & First Contact

Who you already are as an engineer, and who you're about to become. How attacks are built, what makes them legal, virtualization and the lab — then your first authorised packets at a live target.

Foundations Prior-knowledge recap First contact Delivered
S02

Footprinting & Reconnaissance

Build the target picture without touching it — Google dorks, Shodan, WHOIS, certificate transparency, theHarvester, DNS recon and subdomain discovery.

OSINT Shodan · dorks DNS recon Delivered
S03

Scanning & Enumeration

The session where you touch the target and it sees you. Every protocol taught before the scan that attacks it, every scan stepped through packet by packet, and 19 real captures to read alongside — then a working port-scan detection rule you write yourself, and a full team engagement.

Nmap · Wireshark SOC flip Enumeration Delivered
S04

System Hacking I — Vulnerability Analysis & Password Attacks

Turn a version string into an exploit (CVE · CVSS · searchsploit · Nessus), then break authentication against a real domain — NTLM, Kerberos, hashcat, spraying, LLMNR poisoning and Kerberoasting — every attack carried to its auth-log SOC flip and a detection rule you write yourself.

CVE · Kerberoast SOC flip Hash cracking Delivered
S05

System Hacking II — Exploitation, Shells & Payloads

Turn the Session 4 access plan into a shell — manual exploitation then Metasploit, EternalBlue to SYSTEM, bind vs reverse, staged vs stageless payloads with msfvenom, a guided buffer overflow, and a reverse-shell detection rule you write yourself. Every attack carried to its process/network SOC flip, ending in a per-host foothold record.

Metasploit · EternalBlue msfvenom · shells SOC flip Delivered
S06

System Hacking III — Privilege Escalation & CTF Capstone

Turn every low-priv foothold into root and SYSTEM — linPEAS, SUID + GTFOBins, sudo/cron, winPEAS, token abuse (SeImpersonate/potato), and Mimikatz on LSASS — then run two full capstone chains end to end and write the engagement report that closes the offensive half.

Privesc · Mimikatz CTF capstone SOC flip Delivered
S07

Malware Threats & Analysis

Sit in both chairs on one sample — classify malware by behaviour, walk the 5-stage lifecycle, then build a controlled trojan and take it apart: static (hash, VirusTotal, strings, PE imports) and dynamic (isolated sandbox, ProcMon/TCPView). Map every behaviour to ATT&CK and ship the YARA + Sigma rules that catch it, closing with an incident triage report.

Trojans · RATs Static · dynamic · YARA/Sigma Sandbox Delivered
S08

Web Application Hacking & SQL Injection

See the web as three tiers and intercept every request with Burp, then work each injection family to impact — XSS, file upload to shell, command injection, IDOR, and SQL injection from a manual auth bypass through UNION and blind extraction to sqlmap. Every attack ends at its access-log SOC flip, closing with detection rules and a prioritised web-app finding report.

OWASP Top 10 Burp · sqlmap Access-log detection Delivered
S09

Sniffing, MITM, Session Hijacking, Social Engineering & DoS

Attack the four surfaces around the app: sniff and ARP-poison the wire (become the MITM, strip HTTPS), steal and replay a live session (plus CSRF and TCP hijack), phish the human with SET on the psychology of authority/urgency/trust, and flood a service offline (SYN flood, Slowloris). Every attack ends at its detection SOC flip, closing with rules and a four-surface assessment report.

MITM · Hijacking Phishing (SET) · DoS 4-surface detection Delivered
S10

Evasion, Wireless & Emerging Tech

The final session. Evade the defences that watch you (IDS/firewall/honeypot, with fragmentation, decoys and tunnelling), break Wi-Fi (WPA2 handshake capture and offline crack, evil twin), sweep the modern surface (mobile, IoT/OT, cloud misconfig) and audit the cryptography under it all — then tie every session into a defence-in-depth capstone. Graduation.

Evasion · aircrack-ng Mobile · IoT · Cloud · Crypto Defence-in-depth capstone Delivered

About This Course

Who it is for

Students who have completed MCSA, Linux and CCNA and want to move into offensive security or a SOC analyst role. We never re-teach networking or operating system basics — we turn what you already know into attacker leverage.

How it works

Short theory, then straight into your own keyboard. Every student personally runs every attack — no session is spent watching. You work in pairs as a peer-check unit, and every session ends with a written lab report.

What you leave with

A working attack lab, hands-on experience with every technique on the CEH syllabus, a folder of your own lab reports — and the detection angle for each attack, which is what a SOC interview actually asks about.
